In the spirit of actually trying to finish projects rather than just start them, I finally put the finishing touches on an idea that started nine months ago. Originally, I called it 'Humanity's Last Cosmo' as a bit of a tongue-in-cheek reference to one of the common AI benchmarks, Humanity's Last Exam. I wanted to make an app that allowed you to run a set of large language models through popular personality quizzes to find out the answers we really want from AIs—like what kind of Ninja Turtle are you, and which Sex and the City character are you.

I've finally finished that bit, and it's absolutely working. It's totally fun throwing random moral dilemmas at a set of frontier models to see what they answer and why. But I also, and I think in some ways this might be more important, ended up building a set of rankings based around psychometric testing of AI models. We have so many benchmarks and leaderboards on AI's ability to execute tasks, but if we're going to put them in charge of nukes, our cars, and our homes, don't we want to know that they're not fundamentally psychotic?

Well, it turns out that in slightly uplifting news, at least when it comes to frontier models, most of them on measures of narcissism, Machiavellianism, and psychopathy thankfully rate far lower than their human counterparts. Interestingly, outside of some of the less restricted models—which have some very interesting traits—ChatGPT 4.0 stands out. It shows some of the highest levels of those negative traits, along with some very interesting results on other tests like the Big Five personality tests, making it a beast of its own.

It really makes me wonder about all those stories we heard of people ending up in psychotic rages as a result of talking to some of these models. ChatGPT 4.0, by orders of magnitude or by standard deviations, is literally psychotic, narcissistic, and Machiavellian compared to the average human.

The site's up, it's tracking all the frontier models now, and I'm going to keep it updated to make sure we don't have any naughty, devious, dark models sneaking into our homes, cars, lives, or workplaces. Anyway, go have fun. Check out the rankings. Feel free to create your own moral dilemmas, personality quizzes, or anything else and run them up against the best models the world has to offer. Have fun.
